# My Favorite Series: The Dangers in My Heart

**Genre:** Romantic Comedy, Slice of Life, School  

*The dangers in my heart* is a heartwarming anime that tells the story of **Kyotaro Ichikawa**, a socially awkward boy who secretly believes he’s the main character of a dark story, and **Anna Yamada**, a popular girl with a sweet and goofy personality. As they get to know each other, their friendship slowly turns into something more.

# Why I Love This Anime
- It beautifully shows how love can change people for the better.  
- The character development feels natural and touching.  
- The art and music perfectly fit the mood of every scene.  

# Favorite Quote
 “Even the smallest moments can change your heart.”
